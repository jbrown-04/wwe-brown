Synopsis

WrestleMania 39

The main event of WrestleMania 39 featured Undisputed WWE Universal Champion Roman Reigns defending his title against Royal Rumble winner Cody Rhodes in a highly emotional showdown.

Cody Rhodes entered the match with a mission to “finish the story” and capture the championship his father, Dusty Rhodes, never won. The atmosphere was electric as fans witnessed one of the most anticipated main events in modern WWE history.

Despite Cody’s heroic effort, interference from The Bloodline shifted the outcome, allowing Roman Reigns to retain his championship and continue his historic reign.
